What we treat

Specialised care for every stage of childhood

From routine wellness visits to complex gastro concerns, our clinic offers comprehensive paediatric care tailored to your child's needs.

Paediatric Consultation

Thorough, attentive consultations covering all aspects of your child's physical and developmental health — from infancy through adolescence.

Newborn Care

Expert care for newborns — jaundice monitoring, feeding guidance, vaccination planning, and early developmental assessments.

Growth & Development

Tracking developmental milestones, height-weight charts, and early identification of growth concerns to help your child thrive.

Fever & Infection Treatment

Careful evaluation and management of fevers, respiratory infections, viral illness, and common childhood infections.

Child Nutrition Guidance

Evidence-based nutritional advice for every age — from breastfeeding and complementary feeding to balanced diets for growing children.

Paediatric Gastroenterology

Specialised management of reflux, vomiting, loose motions, abdominal pain, constipation, and feeding difficulties in infants and children.
